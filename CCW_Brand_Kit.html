We don't soften the violence. "Slam opponents through flaming tables" not "environmental hazards."

Wink at kayfabe. We're all in on the joke. Self-aware without being ironic or mocking the genre.

Accessible

No gatekeeping. ECW veterans and new fans equally welcome. No "you had to be there" energy.

Hype

Channel promo energy when appropriate. "ARE YOU READY?!" vibes—but earned, not forced.

Card Stat Guide

PWR (Power)

Raw damage output. Higher = harder hits, more devastating weapon attacks.

SPD (Speed)

Movement and attack speed. Higher = faster combos, quicker recovery.

TGH (Toughness)

Damage resistance. Higher = more health, harder to knock down.

HRD (Hardcore)

Weapon proficiency. Higher = better weapon damage, unique weapon moves.
